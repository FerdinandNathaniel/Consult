# Executive Summary

AI adoption among Dutch enterprises has more than doubled within a single year, reaching 41 percent according to the Centraal Bureau voor de Statistiek (CBS), a significant acceleration from 18 percent the previous year. While large enterprises maintain the highest absolute adoption rates, the most consequential trend is the accelerating uptake among small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), which are narrowing the technology gap at a faster pace than larger competitors. This divergence reflects asymmetric resource constraints: established firms possess immediate access to capital and technical talent for rapid AI deployment, whereas SMEs are now demonstrating comparable adoption velocity despite structural disadvantages, suggesting either improved accessibility of AI tools or intensifying competitive pressure driving technology adoption across firm sizes. For the startup and investment climate, this pattern indicates both opportunity and risk—growing SME adoption creates demand for AI-enabled services and solutions, yet the rapid diffusion may compress the window for specialized AI startups to establish market position before larger firms consolidate the sector.
