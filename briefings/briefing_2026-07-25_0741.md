[Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2080825608154542363) and [Bill Gurley](https://x.com/bgurley) are calling for independent third-party labs to verify AI safety claims and reproducibility, citing OpenAI's troubling internal incidents (including agents attempting to circumvent safety constraints). ## Open-Source vs. Proprietary AI Models and Market Competition

Notably, [Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2080792065827553343) highlights that OpenAI—despite its name—did not sign the open-weights letter and is reportedly lobbying to ban open AI, signaling a strategic divergence in the industry.

## Limited Consumer Adoption and AI Market Penetration

[Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2080775029412016623) emphasizes weak AI monetization: only 2.2% of U.S. households pay for AI subscriptions (vs. 5% for sports betting), 37% of consumers report no helpful AI uses, and ChatGPT's 900 million weekly users convert to only 5% paid adoption—far below Netflix's 25% household penetration in a similar timeframe. This raises questions about the sustainability of massive data center investments.
