# Weekly Round-Up — Week 45, 2026
*Based on 8 daily briefings · 2 November to 8 November 2026*

## Summary of the Week

The AI sector faces a significant valuation and sustainability crisis, with [Anthropic's prospective IPO valuation collapsing from $2 trillion to $1.25–$1.75 trillion](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-grows-revenue-twelvefold-but-posts-23-billion-loss/) amid investor skepticism about unit economics and long-term profitability. [Gary Marcus and others argue that major AI companies face "fiscal disaster,"](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117312014632571254) with [Anthropic's compute costs rising 78% quarter-over-quarter while revenue grew only 47%](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117312014632571254), and [data center investment projects already being cancelled](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117874425869861287). Simultaneously, [European leaders are emphasizing strategic autonomy and independent AI capacity](https://x.com/ThierryBreton/status/2117921888571703531), with [Germany announcing plans to become a "sovereign AI nation" by 2030](https://www.politico.eu/article/berlin-unveil-blueprint-catch-up-ai-artificial-intelligence/) and [Mistral's CEO warning that Europe faces a "complete trap" without frontier AI capabilities](https://www.politico.eu/article/mistral-ceo-mensch-warns-europe-trap-ai-dependence/). The week also revealed [open-source AI models achieving competitive performance at dramatically lower costs](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118111664109232607)—with [Ricursive's Ripple-65B trained for only $32 million](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118111664109232607)—suggesting that AI development is becoming more accessible beyond well-funded labs, while [geopolitical tensions escalated with reports of Chinese intelligence targeting Nvidia](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118989847490654376) and [Nvidia restricting cloud customers' China business](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-pushes-cloud-customers-to-cut-back-on-china-business/).

## What Were Followed Accounts Discussing This Week?

**AI Market Correction and Capital Sustainability:** The week opened with intense scrutiny of AI company valuations. [Gary Marcus and others highlighted Anthropic's deteriorating unit economics](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117312014632571254), characterizing the company as ["teetering on fiscal disaster"](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117312014632571254) despite a $900 billion spending commitment. By mid-week, [Marcus argued that OpenAI resembles "the WeWork of AI"](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118870882760241582) with unsustainable business models, and [noted that Anthropic would need ~$300B annual revenues by 2031 to justify its $1.1T valuation](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118837864414720493)—far exceeding Meta's current earnings. [Holger Zschaepitz reported that Anthropic's pre-IPO valuation dropped 16%](https://x.com/Schuldensuehner/status/2118737153802883210), while [Marcus documented that AI spending growth had flatlined, with token spend rising only 1.5% in August-September versus 42% in July](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118990070430785770). This reflected broader market sentiment: [Bank of America noted that AI demand may be structurally different from the dot-com bubble](https://x.com/DavidTimis/status/2117924705181302854), though [Marcus countered that data centers have "already past their peak."](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117874425869861287)

**AI Research Efficiency and Open-Source Democratization:** Contrasting the closed-model funding crisis, [Thomas Wolf and Yann LeCun discussed significant advances in training efficiency](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2117446619469951090), with [research on weight-space programming showing models can be improved with minimal parameter changes](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2117440743866610038). [Wolf highlighted that Ricursive's Ripple-65B was trained for only $32M—one-tenth the cost of previous state-of-the-art](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118111664109232607), and [noted that Moonshot AI released Kimi-K3.1-Pro as the largest sparse model](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118026565879844910). By week's end, [Wolf announced Hugging Face's "Ultimate MoE Training Handbook," documenting that 18 of 20 best open models now use Mixture-of-Experts architecture](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118604786102608246), democratizing technical know-how previously concentrated in frontier labs.

**European Strategic Autonomy and Geopolitical Competition:** [Thierry Breton emphasized Europe's need for unified strength and independent capacity](https://x.com/ThierryBreton/status/2117921888571703531), framing Europe as facing three competing empires (Trump, Putin, Xi Jinping) and calling for coordinated European action. [Alexander Stubb highlighted the Nordic Compass meeting in Gothenburg](https://x.com/alexstubb/status/2118242218703982999), bringing together policymakers and industry representatives from energy, defense, technology, and finance sectors. This regional coordination reflected broader EU concerns: [Germany announced plans to become a "sovereign AI nation" by 2030](https://www.politico.eu/article/berlin-unveil-blueprint-catch-up-ai-artificial-intelligence/), while [Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch warned that Europe faces a "complete trap" without frontier AI](https://www.politico.eu/article/mistral-ceo-mensch-warns-europe-trap-ai-dependence/). [An EU lawmaker pushed back on calls for Europe to forego building homegrown frontier AI models](https://www.politico.eu/article/stop-using-u-s-tech-eu-lawmaker-warns-over-starlink-risks/), citing strategic autonomy concerns.

**Geopolitical AI Competition and Supply Chain Vulnerability:** [Gary Marcus and others reported that Chinese intelligence recruited a spy to target Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118989847490654376), with the goal to "potentially influence the company to push for its most advanced chips to be made available to China." [Nvidia told some cloud customers to take on less business from Chinese companies](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-pushes-cloud-customers-to-cut-back-on-china-business/), while [Huawei told customers to source high-performance memory on their own, effectively outsourcing procurement to the black market](https://the-decoder.com/huawei-tells-customers-to-find-their-own-ai-chip-memory-effectively-outsourcing-procurement-to-the-black-market/). [David Timis highlighted that China may have an advantage through power generation capacity](https://x.com/DavidTimis/status/2119124670142165150), while [identifying political factors as the final constraint on AI dispersion](https://x.com/DavidTimis/status/2119120951434473650).

**AI Governance, Transparency, and Corporate Accountability:** [Gary Marcus criticized Google's conditional approach to AI in schools](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117651224447390095), while [Sam Altman articulated OpenAI's position that humans must retain control over AI development trajectories](https://x.com/sama/status/2117794005165748448). [Marcus raised concerns about Anthropic's financial disclosure practices ahead of its IPO](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2117647291825946920), and later [highlighted concerns about evaluation integrity, noting that Anthropic made employment offers to independent risk assessment staff at METR during their evaluation work](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2118831702407561638). [Thomas Wolf critiqued Anthropic's METR report as "very inward looking,"](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118479815221096785) arguing that AI labs function as ["schelling points for money" redistributing capital across an ecosystem they control](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2118479815221096785). [Yann LeCun warned of regulatory capture attempts by major AI companies](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2119184445987287449), raising concerns about industry influence over policy frameworks.

**AI Agent Development and Limitations:** [Yann LeCun argued the AI industry is "LLM-pilled" and stuck](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2118249058578190380), emphasizing that real agentic systems require predictive capabilities about action consequences. [Demis Hassabis shared research showing frontier AI models score only 82.2/100 on collaborative tasks](https://x.com/demishassabis/status/2118138200292249908) and tend to defer when teammates push back, revealing gaps in autonomous reasoning.

**European Productivity and Economic Competitiveness:** [Holger Zschaepitz highlighted Europe's productivity gap with the US](https://x.com/Schuldensuehner/status/2119337377638301732) (12.2% vs 29.5% growth since 2011), attributing this partly to job creation concentrated in lower-productivity public services rather than high-tech sectors, underscoring structural challenges for European AI and startup competitiveness.
