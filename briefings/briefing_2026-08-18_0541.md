**Massive off-balance-sheet AI commitments inflating corporate debt and interest rates:** Tech giants have accumulated ~$3 trillion in off-balance-sheet AI commitments growing faster than traditional capital expenditure, with nine major companies now competing with the US government for bond market funding.
