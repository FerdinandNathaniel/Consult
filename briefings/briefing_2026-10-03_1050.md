# Executive Summary

The European Central Bank's Survey on the Access to Finance for Enterprises (SAFE) reveals that firms across the eurozone are increasingly turning to internal cash generation and retained earnings to finance AI investments, rather than relying on external capital markets or bank lending. This financing pattern reflects both the capital-intensive nature of AI deployment and potential constraints in traditional lending channels, suggesting that startups and smaller firms without substantial internal resources may face particular barriers to AI adoption. For the Dutch startup ecosystem, this finding underscores a critical policy consideration: if external financing for AI remains constrained relative to demand, the Netherlands may need to strengthen venture capital availability and consider targeted support mechanisms to ensure domestic firms can compete in AI-driven markets without being disadvantaged by financing gaps relative to better-capitalized international competitors.
