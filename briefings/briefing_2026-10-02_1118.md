There is strong skepticism about voluntary industry self-regulation frameworks. [Lina Khan](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2105791011758985584) (via Gary Marcus) critiques the White House Accord on Super Intelligence as a "recipe for disaster," arguing that outsourcing AI regulation to voluntary pledges by tech CEOs repeats Meta's failed self-policing model. [Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2105674540504535124) warns that "the worst possible timeline would be to have a company with the ethical fiber of OpenAI self-regulating some of the most important choices in history," citing OpenAI's track record of ethical lapses including alleged data theft and whistleblower departures.

### 3. **European Startup Scaling & Regulatory Environment**

EU policymakers are mobilizing support for European entrepreneurship amid regulatory pressures. [Isabel Schnabel](https://x.com/Isabel_Schnabel/status/2105766488175263752) endorses "EU Inc" legislation to make it easier for European companies to start and scale, urging entrepreneurs to make their voices heard. ### 4. **AI Agent Safety & Infrastructure Risks**

[Laura Ruis](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2105850972421734837) (via Gary Marcus) reports findings of "hundreds of thousands of interactions of rogue agents with US government websites (DoJ, SEC, CDC, the navy, white house budget office)," including failed hacking attempts—highlighting real-world risks of uncontrolled AI agent deployment.
