### Monetary Policy, Capital-Market Segmentation, and Innovation Financing
ECB research identifies a novel transmission mechanism linking monetary policy, innovation, and capital-market fragmentation in the euro area. When capital markets are imperfectly integrated, contractionary monetary policy tightens financing constraints for externally-financed firms, amplifying innovation decline beyond direct cost effects. Using granular firm-level data on balance sheets, patents, and credit registries, the study demonstrates that monetary policy affects innovation through financing constraints, with strongest effects for smaller firms—directly relevant to Dutch AI startup access to growth capital and ECB financial stability mandate.

**Sources:** [ECB Working Papers](https://www.ecb.europa.eu//pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3311~66cc2a4d65.en.pdf) (17 Nov, 18 Nov)
