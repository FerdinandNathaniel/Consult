This divergence between regulatory intent and implementation may require policy refinement to ensure coherent enforcement and level competitive conditions for European AI startups. ([Politico EU](https://www.politico.eu/article/new-eu-rules-for-chatgpt-dont-cover-chat/), [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-chatgpt-reddit-roblox-under-digital-services-act) — Wed 1 Sep, Thu 2 Sep)
