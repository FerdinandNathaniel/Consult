## AI Development Hype vs. Reality

[Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2106961707554017739) critiques the frontier model release cycle as repetitive marketing: hints of AGI → hype → selective demos → marginal benchmarks → real-world failures → promises the next model will deliver. This pattern undermines credibility and obscures actual capabilities from limitations.

## Open vs. Proprietary AI Models and Market Competition

[Yann LeCun](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2106809439663612341) argues that market forces will drive frontier models toward open-source availability, since distilling expensive frontier models into cheaper versions is economically efficient. He references the Tapestry project as an example of countries building sovereign open models collaboratively.
