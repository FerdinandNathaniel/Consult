## Open-Weight vs. Open-Source AI Models: Definitional Clarity and Market Competition

Experts are debating the distinction between "open-weight" and "open-source" AI models, particularly following Meta's release of Muse Glimmer. [Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2086837786116841715) emphasizes that open-weight models (where weights are publicly available) differ fundamentally from open-source (where full source code, training data, and methodology are transparent), arguing that major media outlets have conflated these terms. [Yann LeCun](https://x.com/ylecun/status/2086973093713457510) frames Meta's open-weight strategy as a competitive reframing—shifting the AI race from "who builds the best models" to "who distributes to the most people," positioning Meta's 3.5 billion users and consumer hardware focus as strategic advantages. [Andrew Ng](https://x.com/AndrewYNg/status/2086845515665166398) and [Thomas Wolf](https://x.com/Thom_Wolf/status/2086814208126054662) endorse Meta's releases, while [Gary Marcus](https://x.com/GaryMarcus/status/2086904814924116315) notes that even with open weights, proprietary "harnesses" (control code) and training data remain closed, limiting true transparency.
